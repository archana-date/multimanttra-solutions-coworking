Frequently Asked Questions

1. Who Can Benefit from the Business Diagnosis Workshop? +
Indian SME founders with annual revenue between ₹50 lakhs and ₹25 crore, who have a team of at least 2 people (or the capacity to hire), but whose business is still heavily owner-dependent and growing at less than 50% annually.
2. Does it need any heavy investment? +
Absolutely not. With little investment, you can start with this.
3. Will I get a personalised action plan? +
You will leave with clarity on three things: what your real problem is, what deserves your attention right now, and what you should stop doing immediately. This is a diagnosis — not a training program. The clarity you get is specific to your business, not generic advice.
4. Do I need to speak or present my business? +
No. You can participate entirely by listening and reflecting. There is no pressure to share sensitive details or speak publicly during the session, but you can share your problem if you are comfortable.
5. What happens after I book my seat? +
You will immediately receive a confirmation on Email and WhatsApp with your Zoom link. You'll also be added to a private WhatsApp group where we'll send your Workbook and the SOP Builder Tool before the session begins.
6. What if I attend and don't find it valuable? +
If you show up, participate, and genuinely don't walk away with clarity — we will refund your ₹99. No questions asked. We're that confident in what this session delivers.
